# Promo storylines

Three candidate films for the Rhythm 2000s promo (35–45 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, music and effects, no voiceover). One gets picked and written up as `SPEC.md`.

## A. Call & Response (recommended)

- **Device / form**: the feature as the hook; cut on the beat.
- **What the viewer sees and feels**: the film plays a round of the game. The XP pointer shows a phrase of pop-ups on the beat, "Your turn" lands, and the windows snap shut on the same beats with Perfect labels. The Mac desktop answers in the same rhythm, then both speed up together into the climax.
- **First two seconds**: Bliss, full frame. On beat 1 an XP error dialog pops up with its original sound, then a second and a third, on beats 2 and 3.
- **Beats**: hook (XP call) → response (windows close, Perfect) → reveal "Rhythm 2000s" → Mac call & response (Dock bounce, Aqua sheets) → the two desktops trade phrases faster and faster → miss gag (one blue screen, one beach ball) → end card.
- **Look**: the original Luna and Aqua chrome at 1.6–2× size on their wallpapers; Tahoma and Lucida Grande on screen; PP NeueBit only on the titles; hard cuts on the bar line.
- **Sound**: a 120 bpm beat built only from the original XP / Mac system sounds placed on the grid (Ding, Start, Pop, Tink, Funk…); half time in the hook, full kit from the reveal, a stop before the blue screen, then the XP Startup chord under the end card.

## B. Log On

- **Device / form**: a moment in someone's day; one continuous take.
- **What the viewer sees and feels**: a sunny British high street around 2000. The camera follows the whale girl into CYBERSPACE CAFÉ, sits her at the beige CRT and pushes through the glass into XP, where a round plays; it pulls out across to the iMac and dives into Aqua, then back out onto the street for the end card.
- **First two seconds**: the street with its zebra crossing and Belisha beacons, the café sign; a door chime.
- **Beats**: street → café → push into the CRT → XP round → pull out, slide to the iMac → Mac round → pull back to the street → end card on the café window.
- **Look**: the real 3D hub (bright, pastel, dreamcore) as the home world, the real game canvases as the two inner worlds.
- **Sound**: street ambience and the XP Logon sound into the beat; the music lives only inside the screens and drops out each time the camera leaves one.
- **Needs**: screen recordings of the hub (walk, sit, push-in) and of both scenes.

## C. Error Storm (the bold one)

- **Device / form**: type carries the argument; one frame that rebuilds.
- **What the viewer sees and feels**: the screen never leaves the desktop. Giant PP NeueBit words hit on every beat (POP. CLOSE. POP. CLOSE.) while real XP dialogs pile up in rhythm until the desktop is buried and turns blue; it reboots into Aqua, where the same rhythm plays as Dock bounces, until the title lands.
- **First two seconds**: one huge word, "POP.", and on the same frame an XP dialog appears beneath it with its sound.
- **Beats**: words + dialogs on the beat → doubling → buried desktop → blue screen silence → Mac chime → Aqua answers → title → end card.
- **Look**: hard colour fields from the two palettes (Luna blue, Start green, Aqua blue) between the desktops; giant pixel type over the real chrome.
- **Sound**: the same system-sound beat as A, but every word is a sound; the silence of the blue screen is the one change.

## Assumptions

40 s, 16:9, music built from the repo's original system sounds (no voice, nothing paid), PP NeueBit on titles. The UI shown comes from the game itself (captures from the dev server), not mock-ups.
